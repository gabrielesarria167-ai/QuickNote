Write the idea down
before it’s gone.
A small Mac app for the moment an idea shows up.
To install, paste into Terminal:
curl -fsSL https://gabrielesarria167-ai.github.io/QuickNote/install.sh | bash
Or download the disk image. Free, for macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel.

How it works

  1. Tap ⌃ Control + ⌥ Option, in any app.
  2. Type the idea in the panel on top.
  3. Press esc. You’re back where you were.

Tap right ⇧ in the panel to flip back through earlier notes. Shortcuts that use ⌃⌥ with another key keep working.

Before you install

Allow it once in Accessibility.

Notes stay on your Mac.

Not notarized: use the command.

macOS asks for Accessibility access so QuickNote can notice ⌃⌥ in other apps; it doesn’t record anything you type. The disk image needs Open Anyway in Privacy & Security the first time. To remove the app, add -s -- --uninstall after bash; your notes are kept.

Try it here: tap ⌃ and ⌥ together, or click them.
