Write the idea down before it’s gone.

QuickNote is a tiny Mac app for the moment an idea shows up. Tap ⌃ Control and ⌥ Option together, in any app, and a small panel opens on top. Type, press esc, and you’re back where you were.

curl -fsSL https://gabrielesarria167-ai.github.io/QuickNote/install.sh | bash

Paste it into Terminal to install. Or download the disk image. Free, for macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel.

How it works

  1. Tap ⌃⌥

    Press Control and Option together and let go, from whatever you’re doing. Shortcuts that use ⌃⌥ with another key keep working as before.

  2. Type the idea

    A small panel opens on top of your work, ready to type. The app you were in stays where it is.

  3. Press esc

    The note is saved and you’re back in your app. Tap right ⇧ in the panel to flip back through earlier notes and add to them.

Before you install

It needs Accessibility access
macOS only lets an app notice keys pressed in other apps once you allow it under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. QuickNote asks the first time it opens. It only watches for ⌃⌥: it doesn’t record or store what you type anywhere else, and every key still reaches your app.
Your notes stay on your Mac
Nothing is uploaded. Notes are saved in ~/Library/Application Support/com.gabrielesarria.quicknote, and the main window lets you search, edit and delete them.
It isn’t notarized by Apple
The Terminal command installs it without warnings. If you use the disk image, the first launch needs System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.
Updating and uninstalling
Run the install command again to update; your notes and the Accessibility setting are kept. To remove the app: curl -fsSL https://gabrielesarria167-ai.github.io/QuickNote/install.sh | bash -s -- --uninstall

QuickNote is free and open source. Source, issues and releases on GitHub.
